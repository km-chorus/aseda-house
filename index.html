Dining

Ghanaian and coastal West African cooking from early morning, and a roof bar that pours until midnight.

  • Kantamanto Table

    06:30 to 22:30

    Breakfast on the terrace, then grilled tilapia with banku, light soup with fufu and jollof with smoked prawns.

  • Tide Bar

    17:00 to midnight

    Sunset on the roof: sobolo spritz, palm wine sour and ginger beer brewed in the house.

Pool, spa and gym

Everything is a short walk across the garden from your door.

  • 25-metre saltwater pool. Shaded until noon, lifeguard from 06:00 to 21:00.
  • The Shea Room spa. Four treatment rooms using shea butter from Tamale and black soap from Kumasi.
  • Gym, open 24 hours. Free weights, rowers and a full cardio line.
